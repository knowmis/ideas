Doctoral Exchange Week

A week of shared research, seminars and cross-lab collaboration on Adaptive & Intelligent Systems, hosted by the KnowMIS lab at the Department of Information and Electrical Engineering and Applied Mathematics (DIEM), University of Salerno.

  • 31 August – 4 September 2026
  • DIEM, University of Salerno, Fisciano (SA), Italy
  • Hosted by the KnowMIS lab
Full program & details

What is IDEAS?

IDEAS (International Doctoral Exchange on Adaptive & Intelligent Systems) is a week for doctoral researchers working on adaptive, intelligent and data-driven systems, built for exchanging ideas and opening the door to future collaboration. Each edition brings visiting PhD students and faculty together with the KnowMIS lab for seminars, hands-on sessions and joint discussion of open research problems.

Doctoral Seminars

PhD students showcase their ongoing work and get feedback and suggestions from peers and faculty.

Invited Talks & Panels

Faculty and students give talks, panels and short speeches on topics of interest for the edition.

Icebreaker Activities

Games and hands-on activities designed to help participants get to know each other from day one.

Cross-lab Networking

Dedicated time to build collaborations that continue after the exchange week ends.
